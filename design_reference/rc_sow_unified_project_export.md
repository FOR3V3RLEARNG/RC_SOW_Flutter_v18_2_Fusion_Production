## 2. Core Operational Architecture
The house is the central operational object. Every record maintains a trustworthy evidence chain from assessment through scope, planning, delivery, quality, close-out, and payment.

### Key Lifecycle Phases:
1. **Scope:** Technical drawing, beneficiary identification, and AI-assisted paper import.
2. **Plan:** Work plan definition, team assignment, and construction scheduling.
3. **Delivery:** Daily site logs, material requests, and consumable logistics.
4. **Quality:** Site visit monitoring, technical checklists, and final inspection.
5. **Close-out:** Notice of Completion and beneficiary acknowledgment.
6. **Finance:** Payment requests, payout logic, and financial settlement.
