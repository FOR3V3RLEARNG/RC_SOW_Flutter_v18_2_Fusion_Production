# Storage Health Threshold Rules
- **Critical Level**: Triggers immediate high-priority alert and automated transfer request proposal.
- **Low Stock Level**: Triggers amber warning on material catalog and adds to weekly logistics briefing.
- **Healthy Level**: Standard operational state.

## Default Material Thresholds (Configurable)
- **Ridge Beams (2x8)**: Critical < 10, Low < 25
- **Zinc Sheets (26ga)**: Critical < 50, Low < 150
- **Hurricane Straps**: Critical < 40, Low < 100
- **Galvanized Nails (Pounds)**: Critical < 20, Low < 50
